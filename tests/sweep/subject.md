# Parser sweep: who a sentence reaches

Generated by `python3 tests/sweep.py`. Each card is added last to a two-player board holding the same 22 blank witnesses on each side (ten 2/2 creatures of different types and colours, six more from 1/1 to 10/10, a creature token, an Equipment, a Treasure token, a Shrine, a Forest and a nonbasic land). A sentence whose subject is a group of permanents is judged by which witnesses change, against the same text with that sentence (or that subject's clause) taken out. "You control" must reach nothing of the opponent's, "your opponents control" nothing of yours, and a subject that names no controller must reach both sides alike. Then each word of the subject is swapped for another of its kind: a word is **unread** when no swap of it changes who is affected. A check is **inert** when the sentence reaches no witness (the board has no chosen types; a mode's sentence is read with that mode picked, a Saga with every chapter reached, and a sentence behind something the site asks with that answered Yes; a sentence that reaches nobody because no witness has its subtype is judged with a witness of that subtype added to each side, and failing that with a subtype of the board standing in for it), or when the card itself is not one of the group "other" leaves it out of. A sentence whose amount is zero on the board ("for each charge counter on this artifact", "where X is the number of experience counters you have") is judged again with some of that amount set up as its own words ask (counters on the card or on the creatures it reaches, experience, commander casts, the Equipment attached, graveyard cards, a permanent of the counted kind, a count typed in); a plain +N/+N that still reaches nobody then is a finding ("amount"). An inert check says nothing either way. A finding means "check this card".
