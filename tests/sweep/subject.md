# Parser sweep: who a sentence reaches

Generated by `python3 tests/sweep.py`. Each card is added last to a two-player board holding the same 16 blank witnesses on each side (ten creatures of different types and colours, a creature token, an Equipment, a Treasure token, a Shrine, a Forest and a nonbasic land). A sentence whose subject is a group of permanents is judged by which witnesses change, against the same text with that sentence (or that subject's clause) taken out. "You control" must reach nothing of the opponent's, "your opponents control" nothing of yours, and a subject that names no controller must reach both sides alike. Then each word of the subject is swapped for another of its kind: a word is **unread** when no swap of it changes who is affected. A check is **inert** when the sentence reaches no witness (the board has no counters, attackers, chosen types or unpicked modes; a sentence that reaches nobody because no witness has its subtype is judged with a witness of that subtype added to each side, and failing that with a subtype of the board standing in for it), or when the card itself is not one of the group "other" leaves it out of; an inert check says nothing either way. A finding means "check this card".
